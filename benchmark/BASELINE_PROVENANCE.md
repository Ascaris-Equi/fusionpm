# Baseline provenance

This inventory is limited to the **15 Fusion-pM baselines**. Fourteen comparator values are transcribed from the published Figure 4 source data of Chu et al. (2022); HLA-Inception is the only baseline independently evaluated for the Fusion-pM comparison. Fusion-pM itself is the proposed model and is therefore not listed as a baseline.

`Source` records where each benchmark value came from. `published` means the value was taken from the immutable Chu et al. source workbook and was not rerun for this revision. `local` means the value was generated by the authors for the Fusion-pM benchmark. The machine-readable [`baseline_versions.csv`](baseline_versions.csv) is authoritative.

Published source: Chu et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence* (2022), DOI [`10.1038/s42256-022-00459-7`](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-022-00459-7); official Figure 4 workbook at TransPHLA-AOMP commit [`3ed2260`](https://github.com/a96123155/TransPHLA-AOMP/blob/3ed2260292934170757507a71e645d0bcadfc44b/Source%20data-Figure%204.xlsx).
